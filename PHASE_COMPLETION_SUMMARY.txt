  • Medical expansion: Tap to expand/collapse animation

  • Communication row: Two buttons side-by-side (50/50)
  • Quick messages method: _showPredefinedMessages()
  • Modal bottom sheet: BottomSheet with ListView of buttons
  • Color hierarchy: Green (primary), Blue (secondary)
